Flagship AI & Engine Architecture Unreal Engine 5 • Blueprints • LLM

UE5 AI-Powered NPC Brain

An advanced, zero-latency AI NPC cognitive framework built in Unreal Engine 5. Players converse naturally with in-game characters using real-time voice input. The system combines local AI inference, live speech recognition (STT/TTS), and dynamic world context injection.

System Architecture & Core Mechanics
  • Voice-to-Text Pipeline (STT): Captures player microphone audio in real-time, converting speech to structured text tokens.
  • Dynamic World Context Injection: Feeds player inventory items, current quest objectives, and nearby actors to the AI prompt buffer.
  • Local AI Processing & Low Latency: Executes lightweight local LLM reasoning without relying on slow external web APIs.
  • Text-to-Speech & State Triggers: AI voice synthesis synchronizes with NPC mouth blendshapes and animates custom reactions.

Ranchland — 3D Farming Simulation & Animal AI

A rich 3D farming adventure bringing simulation mechanics to life in Unreal Engine 5. Combines modular grid building, crop growth stages, crafting recipes, and intelligent autonomous fauna with realistic breeding and pathfinding behavior.

Key Engineering Implementations
  • Behavior Trees & Blackboards: Hierarchical state decisions for animal roaming, hunger states, and rooster territorial awareness.
  • Dynamic Nest Searching & Breeding: AI hens autonomously discover player-crafted nests to lay eggs.
  • Modular Building Hammer: Real-time construction system for walls, doors, floors, and machinery using modular snap points.
  • Multi-stage Crop Growth: Agricultural growth pipeline with soil tilling, hydration checks, and harvesting yields.
